Siddhi's Creative Process

Siddhi has a background in graphic design and mainly works in visual communication, social media, and branding. Her projects are usually client-based and can take around a month depending on the scale of the project

1 - Conversation

Conversation Comes first

Conversation is one of the most important parts of the creative process. Before designing anything, she talks with the client to understand their goals, aesthetic preferences, customers, and background.

She is especially interested in the client’s cultural background. She asks where they come from and what stories or experiences are meaningful to them, then looks for ways to bring those elements into the brand.

2 - Research

After the initial conversation, Siddhi researches the client’s aesthetic and tries to understand their customers. She looks at what kind of visual language would make sense for both the client and the people they want to reach.

3 - Reference

Gathering References

Siddhi collects references online and organizes them into different visual categories. From these references, she creates mood boards and begins making design decisions about the overall direction of the brand.

4 - Sketchcing and Visualizing

Turning Idea into Visuals

Once the direction becomes clearer, Siddhi starts sketching and visualizing different ideas. She develops examples that show how the brand could look and presents these possibilities to the client.

5 - Pitching Client

Presenting the Direction

She presents visual examples and her own design opinions to the client. The goal is not only to show a finished design, but also to explain why a certain direction makes sense for the brand.

 

Building the Whole Brand

Depending on the client’s budget and needs, she can develop an entire brand system rather than just a logo or visual identity.

Digital and Physical Elements

  • Logo
  • Scent
  • Cutlery
  • Social Media
  • Uniform
 

Culture as Part of Branding

One thing she values is bringing cultural content into branding. Rather than creating a brand based only on visual trends, she tries to understand the client’s background and use it to create a more personal brand story.

This can be difficult because clients sometimes want to reproduce something they have already seen elsewhere. Siddhi tries to balance those references with elements that feel more specific to the client.
